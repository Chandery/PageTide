Let the right papers
find you.

PageTide learns your research profile, ranks new papers on your own computer, and keeps the ones you save in a library you own.

A research assistant for every step of your reading

Each morning a short list of new papers, ranked against your profile, each with a one-line reason. One optional AI call previews up to 20 picks at once.

Every paper you save or mark Not interested refines your profile, so it sharpens the more you use it. It stays a short list of plain keywords, yours to adjust at any time.

Saved papers become a list and a graph of related work. Open-access PDFs are archived on your computer, and nothing is reorganized until you confirm.

Read the PDF inside PageTide, select a sentence, then translate it or ask about it. The answer appears beside the page.

Import PDF files or folders (subfolders become categories), or a Zotero collection exported as BibTeX, RIS or CSL JSON.

What you saved, what you skipped and which directions grew this week, so the shape of your reading is visible.

Change a keyword and today’s five picks re-rank. Click a keyword to cycle strong, weak and off, or add your own.

More papers is not the problem. Finding yours is.

About 20,000 new papers appear every day1. A handful of them matter to your work, and no one can read a feed that long. Most tools answer with a bigger feed. PageTide answers with a smaller one.

It learns a research profile from what you mark useful or not relevant, then ranks each day’s new papers on your own computer. The profile is a list of plain words you can read, correct and delete. AI is optional, and its job is to explain a paper to you, not to choose it for you. Unlike a chat-based AI research assistant, PageTide does not ask you to describe what you want each time. It reads your feedback, keeps a research profile you can edit, and turns it into paper recommendations that improve the more you use it.

Your library, your PDFs, your notes and your profile stay on your computer. There is no account, no server and no telemetry.

First launch: the app is not signed

PageTide is an independent open-source project with no paid code-signing certificate, so your system will warn you the first time. This is expected.

macOS

Unzip, drag PageTide to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open. Or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

If macOS says the app is damaged, run this in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PageTide.app

Windows

When SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is per-user and needs no administrator rights.

Requirements

  • macOS on Apple Silicon (M1 or later) or Intel.
  • Windows on x64.

Keys for outside services

The core (picking, the profile, the library) works offline with no key. Optional services use your own key, stored on your computer.

  • DeepSeek for the AI features (previews, questions, suggestions). Optional.
  • OpenAlex for a wider paper source. Optional.
  • Baidu Scholar needs a Qianfan key.
  • arXiv needs no key.

Contributions welcome

PageTide is a research assistant built in the open. Bug reports, feature ideas, documentation and translation fixes, and pull requests are all welcome, and small fixes count as much as new features. For a bigger change, please open an issue first so we can agree on the direction.
